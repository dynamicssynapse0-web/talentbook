FamillyWorker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

FamillyWorker is an application designed to help users create, present and connect professional and sports profiles.

1. Information We May Collect

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • WhatsApp number when provided;
  • profile photo;
  • country, city and nationality;
  • gender and date of birth when provided;
  • professional information;
  • sports experience information;
  • job or sports position;
  • photos associated with profiles and experiences;
  • information required to manage subscriptions or purchases.

2. How We Use Information

  • to create and manage your account;
  • to display your professional or sports profile;
  • to allow users to view published profiles;
  • to manage professional and sports experiences;
  • to manage purchases, subscriptions and paid features;
  • to maintain the security and functionality of the application;

3. Photos and User Content

When you upload a profile photo or an experience photo, FamillyWorker may store and display that content as part of the application's functionality.

You must only upload content that you have the right to use and share.

FamillyWorker uses third-party technical services, including Supabase, for authentication, storage and management of certain application data.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including FuturePay.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information that you choose to make public in your profile may be visible to other FamillyWorker users.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide FamillyWorker features, comply with legal obligations and maintain service security.
